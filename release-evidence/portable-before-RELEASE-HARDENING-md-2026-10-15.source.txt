### Correctness and resource changes

- Receiver validates bounded groups and reuses BlockStore.writeBlocks: payload
  flush precedes one durable receipt record, followed by one ACK per group.
  Defaults: 64 blocks / 75 ms. No verification was disabled.
- Replaced the unbounded per-message Promise chain with one commit task and one
  timer. At most 192 encoded blocks (about 11.3 MiB) are retained by that queue,
  including active writes. Other bounded decoder/store buffers are additional.
- Short groups flush on a deadline, including when the sender's 96-block window
  is smaller than a configured group. Stop drops queued work, awaits an active

  a full fragmented range array. ACK buffering is capped. Authorization remains
  checked at arrival, and early/duplicate manifest controls cannot grow a queue.
- Sender rejects repeated/overlapping resume pages and ACKs for unsent blocks.
  Fixed a lost-failure wait when a paused sender failed before waiting began.
- Worker checks journal length after flush before reporting commit success.
  Unexpected-size diagnostics preserve expected/actual lengths.
